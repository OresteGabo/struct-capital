# Struct Capital — Vision

## North star
**One clearer view of investments, across brokers.** Struct Capital aims to help people understand and manage Rwandan Treasury bonds and listed equities without forcing them to switch brokers or depend on an institutional integration.

## The investor problem
An investor may use multiple licensed intermediaries and receive separate allocation notices, contract notes, statements, and income records. Documents differ in layout, fees, terminology, and frequency. Investors need a coherent view of holdings, transactions, coupons, dividends, costs, and upcoming events.

## Our approach
- **Independent first:** usable through supported document imports and, where implemented, manual entry even when no broker collaborates.
- **Evidence-based:** preserve document provenance; distinguish confirmed records from estimates, projections, and unverified prices.
- **Understandable:** translate complicated records into transparent, understandable portfolio information.
- **Investor-controlled:** protect financial data and avoid claiming access to broker accounts without authorization.
- **Expandable:** build reusable parsing and reporting foundations that might eventually serve institutions.

## What we are not today
Struct Capital is not a broker, exchange, custodian, bank, trading venue, or investment adviser. It does not currently execute orders or hold customer securities or funds. The website must not imply that it does.

## Who we serve
**Initially:** individual investors in Rwanda who own Treasury bonds or RSE-listed shares, whether they use one broker or several. **Potentially later:** licensed brokers, other financial institutions, and financial educators, subject to validated needs and appropriate agreements.

## Long-term ambition
Build a trusted, sustainable software business with the option to offer premium investor tools, document-processing technology, institutional reporting, and consent-based integrations. A separately authorized brokerage could be considered much later, but is **not an inevitable destination** and is not part of the current product.

## What success means
- Investors can accurately consolidate supported investments.
- Financial calculations and import results are verifiable and correctable.
- Users understand what the platform does and does not do.
- Retention, support quality, import accuracy, and eventually sustainable revenue improve.
- Expansion is driven by actual user and institutional demand, not assumptions.

## Public messaging guardrails
Say **“supports documents from brokers”** only when format support is verified. Say **“partners with brokers”** only after a verified formal relationship. Never invent customer counts, licenses, integrations, testimonials, or app availability.

# Compliance and Public Claims Register

> This document is a planning aid, **not legal advice or a compliance certification**. Verify applicable Rwandan law and current regulator requirements with qualified professionals before regulated activities or public legal assertions.

## Present positioning
Struct Capital is described as **independent investment portfolio management software**. Do not present it as a licensed brokerage, custodian, bank, securities exchange, or investment adviser unless facts and authorizations change and are verified.

## What the website may explain
- Users invest through their chosen authorized financial intermediaries.
- Struct Capital helps organize information from supported documents and verified manual workflows.
- Supporting a broker's PDF format is not evidence of a formal relationship.
- Direct broker synchronization is a future possibility requiring agreements and appropriate customer authorization.

## Claims requiring evidence and approval
| Claim | Required verification |
|---|---|
| “Partnered with Broker X” | Signed, active agreement and approved public naming |
| “Supports Broker X documents” | Tested formats and limitations; no partnership implication |
| “Available on App Store/Google Play” | Active approved listing and link |
| “N registered users” | Accurate source, definition, timestamp, privacy review |
| “Live prices” | Authorized source, update frequency, market-data rights |
| “Secure/encrypted” | Verified scope and implementation; avoid blanket guarantees |
| “Licensed/regulated” | Exact entity, license scope, current regulator confirmation |

## Legal review topics
- Rwanda personal data protection and privacy obligations.
- Consumer protection, advertising accuracy, website disclosures, and terms.
- Financial promotion and investment advice boundaries.
- Copyright and permitted use of broker documents/logos/market data.
- Cross-border processing, vendors, analytics, and retention.
- Future regulated brokerage, trading, custody, and financial institution software requirements.

## Education versus advice
Educational explanations of bonds, coupons, dividends, and portfolio concepts must not promise returns or provide unqualified personalized recommendations. Projections need visible assumptions and limitations.

## Approval process
Before publishing legal or regulated claims: identify the claim, evidence, responsible reviewer, approval date, and required recheck cadence. If evidence is missing, remove or soften the claim rather than fabricate support.

## Future partnership data
Broker integrations require documented contracts, customer authorization where applicable, scoped access, revocation, and appropriate lawful processing. Broker customer data is not automatically Struct Capital data.

## Website legal drafts (2026-10-30)

`/privacy` and `/terms` are explicit review drafts, always noindex and absent from the public sitemap, even when site indexing is enabled. Footer links identify them as drafts. They describe verified website behavior and unresolved review items; they are not an approved app policy, binding-contract claim, or compliance certification. The contact permission checkbox is for replying only, not acceptance of these drafts.

Operator identity, approved contacts, providers, processing locations, retention, applicable legal bases/rights, app data handling, service conditions and final legal language still require qualified review. No governing-law, liability, response-deadline or encryption promise was invented. A reference for that review is the Rwanda Data Protection and Privacy Office’s [organization guidance](https://dpo.gov.rw/assets/documents/organization-faqs.pdf); it is not evidence that Struct Capital satisfies any obligation. Current applicability must be assessed for actual operations.

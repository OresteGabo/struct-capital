# Product Strategy

## Positioning
Struct Capital is an independent investment portfolio management software platform for investors in Rwanda. Its key distinction is **cross-broker visibility without requiring direct broker partnerships**.

## Personas and jobs to be done
| Persona | Problem | Product response | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual bond investor | Track allocations, coupons, maturity, and costs | Supported document import, schedules, holdings | Verify in KMP/backend |
| Equity investor | Track quantities, acquisition fees, dividends, performance | Contract-note import and portfolio records | Verify in KMP/backend |
| Multi-broker investor | Consolidate separate records | Broker-independent normalized portfolio | Verify supported formats |
| Prospective institutional partner | Reduce manual document/reporting work | Future processing/reconciliation SaaS | Interviews and paid pilots |

## Value propositions
1. Keep investments from different brokers in one portfolio view.
2. Extract structured information from **supported** PDFs rather than retyping everything.
3. Explain coupon income, fees, portfolio composition, and future events transparently.
4. Continue working without a direct broker connection.
5. Keep financial records traceable to original evidence.

## Current distribution strategy
Start with a credible public website, genuine app screenshots, FAQs, helpful financial education, and verified app-store links once published. Collect feedback and validate retention before significant advertising expenditure.

## Potential revenue models — hypotheses, not commitments
- Optional advanced individual-investor analytics or reporting.
- Paid institutional document processing, reconciliation, or reporting.
- Enterprise APIs and consent-based account integrations under agreements.

Do not introduce pricing, subscriptions, or paywalls until product-market evidence supports them. A target of 2–5 million RWF in monthly net business profit is an aspiration, **not** a forecast.

## Validation questions
- Can users import documents accurately without support?
- Do they return to check holdings and upcoming income?
- Which unsupported broker formats are requested most?
- Which workflows are painful for broker employees?
- Would institutions pay for a pilot, and for which measurable outcome?

## Metrics to define and measure (no invented baselines)
Activation, successful imports, import correction rate, active users, retention, feedback themes, support burden, conversion if monetization is introduced, institutional interviews, paid pilots, MRR, costs, and net profit. Publish aggregate statistics only after privacy and accuracy review.

## Non-goals for the website
No account trading, custody, personalized investment advice, authenticated investor dashboards, unverified partner logos, or invented market-data feeds.

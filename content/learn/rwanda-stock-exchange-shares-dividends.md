## Shares represent ownership
A share is an ownership interest in a company. CMA Rwanda’s [capital-market FAQ](https://www.cma.rw/faq) distinguishes shares from lending through bonds. The Rwanda Stock Exchange is a securities marketplace; it is different from a company whose shares are listed there, and from a broker acting on an investor’s instructions.

## Income is different from price movement
A dividend is a distribution a company may make to shareholders. Not every company pays dividends, and payments can change or stop. Separately, a share’s market value can rise or fall. Neither dividends nor capital gains should be assumed or guaranteed. [Investor.gov’s stocks introduction](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/stocks) covers the general ownership and income concepts.

## Check the company’s announcement
For a particular dividend, consult the issuer’s official announcement for its amount, eligibility dates and payment arrangements. This guide does not provide a current dividend calendar, dealing rules, fees or tax treatment.

## Editorial outline — no current fee schedule
This page is not a fee quotation or a completed guide. Current commissions, exchange charges, taxes, minimums and negotiated broker terms have not been verified for publication. No fee amounts or percentages are presented.

## Evidence needed before completion
- A dated, currently effective official exchange or regulator schedule, with its scope established.
- Relevant broker-specific terms, including minimums and any charges not covered by that schedule.
- Qualified review of any proposed tax explanation, distinguishing law from a broker’s document layout.
- An original, permission-cleared illustration that contains no customer information.

The guide must distinguish a maximum permitted fee from an actual fee charged and identify which date and transaction type each source covers.

## Questions to take to your broker
Ask for an itemized explanation of a quoted or charged total, its calculation basis and the applicable written terms. Keep that explanation with your transaction evidence. Do not assume a percentage found in an old document still applies.
